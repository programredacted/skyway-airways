{# The aircraft is drawn the way printed seat charts draw it: a pale silhouette sitting behind the cabin, carrying the shape while the seats stay the only high-contrast thing on the page. That choice is what makes the geometry easy. An outlined airframe has to meet the cabin walls exactly or it reads as sprouting from a spine, which forces the cabin to shrink until the wings fit. A silhouette passes behind an opaque cabin, so nothing has to line up and the wings are free to be as large as the shape needs — including running off the edge of the column, which the surrounding box clips. #} {% macro nose() -%} {# Kept deliberately shallow.
